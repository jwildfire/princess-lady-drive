Princess Lady Drive

Drive Queen Rosie around a town where everything is free.

  • 🚗“You can drive around”
  • 🚪“You can get out of the car”
  • 🎁“Everything’s free”
  • 👑“A pretty, like, queen”

Game design by a 4-year-old. Grown-ups: the ? button explains the controls.

Where to?

For grown-ups

  • Tap a spot to go there. Hold a finger down to keep going toward it. Tap a shop to head to its door.
  • Walk in a door to go inside. Inside, walk up to anything to take it. Sit in seats by stopping on them. Walk out the bottom door, or press Go out.
  • The pink button gets in or out of the car. Tap any parked car (the Car Lot has lots) to drive that one. If your car is far away, Get in brings it over.
  • The Halloween store’s things go in Rosie’s bag. Walk into the castle to decorate it. The castle has a pool.
  • Walk or drive into the soccer ball and golf balls to kick them. Drive around the race track to count laps.
  • The 🗺️ button jumps to any place. Keyboard: arrows or WASD to move, Space to get in or out, H to beep or sparkle.
